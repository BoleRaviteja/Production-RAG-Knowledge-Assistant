ACME TECH - NEW EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING GUIDE

1. YOUR FIRST DAY
Arrive at 10 AM at the front desk and ask for the People Team. You will receive your laptop,
ID card, and access to email and Slack. Your buddy will take you for lunch.

2. THE FIRST WEEK
Day 1: IT setup and security training.
Day 2: Meet your team and review the 30-60-90 day plan with your manager.
Day 3 to 5: Read the engineering handbook and set up your development environment.
Complete the mandatory Information Security and Code of Conduct courses within 7 days.

3. PROBATION
The probation period is 90 days. During probation you are eligible for casual and sick
leave but not work from home. A review meeting with your manager happens at day 45 and
day 90.

4. SALARY AND PAYROLL
Salary is credited on the last working day of each month. Payslips are available on the HR
portal. Reimbursement claims must be submitted by the 25th to be included in that month's
payroll. The company provides health insurance covering the employee, spouse, and up to
2 children from the first day of employment.

5. LEARNING BUDGET
Every employee has an annual learning budget of 40000 rupees for courses, books, and
certifications. Submit the receipt through the HR portal for reimbursement. Cloud
certifications such as AWS and Kubernetes exams are covered in full.

6. WHO TO ASK
IT problems: it-support@acmetech.example or Slack #it-help.
HR and policy questions: hr-help@acmetech.example.
Access to cloud accounts: raise a ticket in the "Access Request" Jira project.
